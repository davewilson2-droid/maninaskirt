For help with Remind Me Yesterday, open TestFlight, select the app, and choose Send Beta Feedback. Describe what you expected and what happened. Avoid including passwords, connection keys or sensitive source details.

AI chat and source interpretation need an internet connection. Existing saved plans remain on your device. Phone alerts require notification permission; check Settings → Notifications in the app if a saved task has no alert.

This is a private trial. Calendar and email changes are picked up when the app checks its connected sources; it does not promise continuous background monitoring.
