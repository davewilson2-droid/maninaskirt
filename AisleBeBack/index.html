A quick driving lesson.

  1. Hold your device in landscape. Choose which half steers on the opening screen, then start a race, a solo run or free roam.
  2. Swipe sideways on the steering half. Swipe up on the speed half to drive forward, or down to brake and reverse. The whole half is active; small swipes give gentle control.
  3. Collect the five gold rings. Park in a checkout box and lift your speed thumb to finish.

Prefer tilting the phone? Enable tilt steering in Controls & settings and centre it at a comfortable holding angle. Touch steering stays available.

A few aisle-side questions.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

The game itself works offline and has no account or sign-in. Apple’s TestFlight needs internet access to install and update beta builds.

How do I send feedback?

Use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight, or email hello@maninaskirt.com. Tell us your device, what happened and what you expected. Please avoid including private information in screenshots.

What happens to my scores and motion data?

Best times and settings stay on your device. Tilt steering uses device motion locally. The game has no ads or analytics SDKs. Apple handles TestFlight diagnostics and feedback separately. Read the privacy policy.
