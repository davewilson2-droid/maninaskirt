This policy describes the current Aisle Be Back iPhone and iPad beta, developed by maninaskirt (Dave Cartwright-Wilson). Updated 21 October 2026.

The game works locally

The game does not require an account and can be played offline. It has no advertising or cross-app tracking. Optional anonymous gameplay analytics and developer announcements use a network connection when available.

Scores and settings

Your best solo and race times, rival difficulty, steering preference, tilt sensitivity and sound preference are stored in the app’s local storage. Best-time records remain on your device. Anonymous analytics can include individual trip duration and outcome, item count, chosen mode, control settings, app version and operating-system name. Removing the app removes its local app data; device backups and restores are managed through your Apple settings.

Optional tilt steering

If you enable tilt steering, the game reads the device’s motion/gravity signal to steer the scooter. It processes this on the device and does not store a motion history or transmit motion readings. You can turn tilt off in Controls & settings and use touch steering instead.

Anonymous gameplay analytics

We use TelemetryDeck to understand sessions, control choices, where shopping trips finish or stop, and use of checkouts and the free-roam finale. Events contain a random identifier for the current app session. We do not send a persistent player or device identifier, name, email, precise location, shopping route, raw touch input or motion readings. This means we analyse sessions rather than follow individual players across visits.

You can turn sharing off in Controls & settings → Privacy & licences. Turning it off stops future collection and clears events waiting on the device. Gameplay remains available offline. Network requests necessarily pass through the provider’s infrastructure; see TelemetryDeck’s privacy information. TestFlight builds send test-mode events, separate from production analytics.

Developer announcements

The app reads public developer notices from maninaskirt.com/AisleBeBack/notices/feed.json. Notices are cached on your device and can be dismissed; dismissal is saved locally for that notice revision. Announcement checks send no player account or persistent identifier. Website hosting may process ordinary connection details to serve the feed. These are in-app notices, not push notifications.

Apple TestFlight

Apple operates the beta installation and feedback service. TestFlight can provide us with information about beta sessions, crashes, device and operating-system details, and feedback you choose to submit. Feedback sent through TestFlight is also provided to Apple. This is separate from the game’s local scores and controls.

Apple’s TestFlight tester information explains the service and what is shared. See also Apple’s privacy information. Please check screenshots and attachments before sending feedback, as they may contain information you did not intend to share.

Support email

If you email hello@maninaskirt.com, we receive your address, message and any attachments you send. We use this to answer your request and investigate reported problems. Do not send passwords, financial information or other sensitive information. Contact us at the same address for questions about information you have sent or to request its removal.

This website

These game and privacy pages do not add advertising cookies or analytics scripts. Our website hosting infrastructure may process normal connection information, such as IP address and request details, to deliver and secure the website. Following an external link takes you to that provider’s service and privacy terms.

Changes and contact

If a later build changes how the game handles data, we will update this page. For game support or privacy questions, contact hello@maninaskirt.com.
