A FEW THINGS BEFORE DEPARTURE

Good questions.

Do I need an iPad?

No. The web version runs in a modern browser on a suitable phone, tablet or computer. Use a device you already have, or build a dedicated display. You do not need to buy the Apple app to subscribe on the web.

Which buses and Tube services are included?

The bus and Underground boards use TfL arrival information for London. They are not a nationwide bus service. Railway boards show UK rail departures.

Can I try it before paying?

You can explore the read-only demo. Creating a web account is free; your own Personal boards unlock after a confirmed £2.99 monthly subscription payment. There is no free subscription trial.

What if I already subscribe in the app?

Use your existing app and portal account. Apple manages App Store subscriptions; PayPal manages web subscriptions. You do not need to buy a second subscription for the same account’s existing paid access. Contact us if you need help finding the right login.

How do I cancel?

Open Web billing in your account, or manage your automatic payment in PayPal. Cancelling stops future payments and keeps access until your paid period ends. Your saved boards remain. App Store subscriptions are managed separately through Apple.

Looking for a departure board for your desk or shelf?

Destination Board is a software alternative to buying a dedicated departure board. Turn a suitable tablet or computer screen into a live train departure board for home, with London bus and Tube boards too. Bring your own screen and subscribe for £2.99/month; a screen, case and stand are not included.

Is this a physical departure board?

This is the software. You supply the screen, internet connection and any case or stand. Live information can change or be delayed—always check your journey before travelling.
