About me

maninaskirt was born in a bedroom during an extended period of recovery following knee surgery—hence the skirt.

With too much time on my hands, I turned to coding to solve the small, frustrating problems that bothered me. I soon realised that some of the solutions I was making for myself and my family might be useful to other people too, so I began releasing them through the App Store.

My apps may not always be 100% perfect. They are living products, built with care and continually improving. I welcome honest feedback, ideas and suggestions from the people who use them.

My perspective comes from years spent across a large surgery, urgent and out-of-hours care, 111, intermediate care, specialist services and unusual clinical environments. That breadth matters: technology only works when it understands the people, pressures and handovers around it.

Alongside clinical work, I have led digital change, contributed to national digital discussions, supported rapid service redesign and evaluated new technology through the lenses of clinical safety, usability and workflow.

I also build. From identifying a need and shaping the user experience to prototyping, debugging, testing and shipping, I enjoy the full path from an untidy problem to something useful in someone’s hand.
