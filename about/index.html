the story behind the name

Why
maninaskirt?

maninaskirt was born in a bedroom during an extended period of recovery following knee surgery—hence the skirt (and now the name).

I’m Dave, a doctor with an interest in digital health and building useful things. With too much time on my hands, I turned to coding to solve the small, frustrating problems that bothered me. I soon realised that some of the solutions I was making for myself and my family might be useful to other people too, so I began releasing them through the App Store.

My apps are living products, built with care and continually improving. I welcome honest feedback, ideas and suggestions from the people who use them.
